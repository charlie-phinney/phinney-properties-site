# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Twin Cities rental owners, often 45 and up, deciding whether to trust someone with their property. They read on a laptop at home or a phone, in daylight, unhurried and a little wary. Secondary user: Nancy Phinney, a non-technical editor who manages every word and photo through /admin.

## Product Purpose

Website for Phinney Properties (phinneyproperties.com): residential property management in the Twin Cities. Success = an owner understands in ten seconds who Nancy is and what she takes care of, and can send a note in one step. Nancy can change anything herself without breaking the page.

## Brand Personality

Steady, neighborly, exact. One local person who picks up the phone, not a corporate portal.

## Anti-references

- Template property-management sites: stock handshakes, portal buttons, walls of county lists
- Navy and gold "real estate" branding
- Anything Nancy cannot edit herself through /admin

## Design Principles

1. Trust through plainness: short true statements, no inflated numbers or badges.
2. Editor-proof: every section survives blank fields, odd pastes and reordering.
3. One page, orderable: sections can be hidden or moved; each stands alone at any width.
4. Her homes are the imagery: real photos of her properties, never stock people.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Large tap targets, large readable text (older audience), reduced-motion honored, semantic landmarks, labeled form fields.
